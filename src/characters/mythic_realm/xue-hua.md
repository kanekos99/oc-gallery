<!-- section: introduction -->

The youngest of the four remaining nine-tailed fox spirits left in the world, Xue Hua is a playful trickster with a remarkable talent for shape-shifting. A free-spirited individual who does not like to be tied down to anyone or anywhere, she roams the lands without a care in the world, only seeking to amuse herself at every opportunity.<br><br> 

She shares a somewhat rocky but ultimately amicable relationship with her fellow nine-tailed fox, [Qiu Ye](/oc-gallery/characters/qiu-ye), who views her as a younger sister.<br><br>  

Xue Hua has few martial skills to to speak of, thus she largely gets by using her magic and shape-shifting abilities. She enjoys provoking others, but is quick to flee from a fight as soon as she realises she is at a disadvantage. She recognises that she is weak compared to the other nine-tailed fox spirits, and admits that she is a bit of a coward, thus she is not above using manipulation and blackmail to get what she wants.<br><br>  

She easily charms others to become her allies, yet her independent and willful nature makes it difficult for her to trust anyone fully or form a genuine relationship with them. She yearns for someone who can devote themselves to her, although she is reluctant to reciprocate in turn.

<!-- sub-section: past -->

<div class="alert alert-warning"><b>Warning:</b> Mentions of dark topics like child sacrifice and suicide ideation</div>

One snowy day, a little fox girl was born to the fox spirit concubine of a rich merchant. Her mother died shortly after her birth and she was disregarded completely by her father, left to be raised as a nameless servant for his real family.<br><br> 

When a demonic entity started to terrorise the village they lived in, she was chosen to become a sacrifice to appease it. Said demonic entity turned out to be a nine-tailed fox spirit, who was more irritated by the sacrifice, murdering all the villagers involved (including the girl’s father) and rescuing the girl.<br><br>

Since the girl had no family left to care for her, the fox spirit, [Qiu Ye](/oc-gallery/characters/qiu-ye), took her under his wing, naming her Xue Hua after her pure white hair and fur. He taught her to read and write, as well as how to use magic to protect herself. They travelled together for a while, and Xue Hua grew incredibly attached to her savior.<br><br>

However, Qiu Ye led a dangerous life — he made many enemies in his violent crusade to purge the corrupt and seek justice for the weak. Not wanting Xue Hua to be harmed because of him, he eventually left her in the care of a kind elderly couple in an isolated village. Unable to understand why Qiu Ye had left her behind, Xue Hua felt frustrated and abandoned.<br><br>

Nevertheless, life in the village was peaceful and she finally had a loving family. She was content to stay for the time being, although she never stopped training and trying to grow stronger so that she could some day become as strong as Qiu Ye, whom she greatly idolized.<br><br>

Yet even her new life would fall apart one day when a cursed plague swept through their village, turning the infected into mindless zombies. Xue Hua was forced to put down her own adoptive parents, and the act of killing two of the most important people to her led to her gaining her first two tails.<br><br>

As most of the villagers had been turned, they were subsequently killed as well by Xue Hua. Stricken with grief and rage, she tracked down the shaman who had brought the plague upon her village, only for the shaman to reveal that he had a cure all along in an attempt to get Xue Hua to spare his life. Horrified and furious, Xue Hua killed him on the spot, earning her a third tail. Before the shaman died, he taunted Xue Hua and said that she had become a monster.<br><br>

Unable to return to the village out of guilt for what she had done, Xue Hua tried to track down Qiu Ye again as she believed that he would understand what she had been through. Yet it would take several years for her to find him again, and during that time, having to survive on her own, her powers continued to grow stronger. She would kill indiscriminately out of fear, out of anger, doing whatever it took to protect herself.<br><br>

By the time she was reunited with Qiu Ye, she now had six tails. The older fox spirit expressed sympathy towards her plight, mourning that he was unable to prevent her from falling down the same dark path that he took. Xue Hua recognised at last the cost that came with a nine-tailed fox spirit's powers. Yet, out of desire for protection and companionship, and with nowhere else left to go, she simply chose to follow after Qiu Ye again.<br><br>

This time, Qiu Ye accepted her presence, perhaps out of a sense of responsibility. As Xue Hua aided him in taking down many of his enemies, they grew closer. Xue Hua found herself becoming infatuated with Qiu Ye, although he would constantly spurn her affections, maintaining that he still saw her as a child.<br><br>

This annoyed Xue Hua immensely and she grew more reckless in an attempt to prove herself to him. Yet, the more she tried to impress Qiu Ye, the more distant he became. In fact, the older fox spirit appeared more and more despondent as the days wore on, lamenting that their actions were futile and that the more they tried to help, the more suffering they would cause.<br><br>

Xue Hua never believed in Qiu Ye's mission, nor did she share his sense of justice. She was, to put it simply, merely along for the ride. She was content as long as they had a good time and lived to see another day, because it was impossible to expect anything more. Thus, she could not understand the source of his misery. Until one day, Qiu Ye disappeared and abandoned her once more.<br><br>

It did not take her as long to track him down again this time. By then, Xue Hua had eight tails. When she demanded to know why Qiu Ye had left her behind, the older fox spirit snapped at her and told her he had always found her irritating and that he hated her. He proclaimed that if she attempted to follow him again, he would kill her. Outraged and heartbroken, Xue Hua refused to leave and Qiu Ye attacked her.<br><br>

She found herself fending him off with surprising ease, and she ended up nearly killing him before stopping herself just in time. Yet Qiu Ye begged for Xue Hua to kill him, expressing that he was proud of her and that she could gain her last tail to become a fully-fledged nine-tailed fox if she took his life.<br><br>

Disgusted that Qiu Ye would use her as a means to end his own life, Xue Hua refused and left.<br><br>

For several decades to come, she would not see him again, nor hear about his exploits, which did make her wonder sometimes if he was still alive. But at last, it did not seem to matter to her anymore. Xue Hua would go on to earn her final tail by herself. 

<!-- sub-section: present -->

Xue Hua never stayed in one place for long, not even using her true appearance most of the time. She had no more family, nor a home she could return to. Sometimes it bothered her, but other times, she had already resigned herself to the fact that such was the way nine-tailed foxes lived. They were truly monsters, after all, and it was only in becoming a monster had she managed to survive.<br><br> 

Many years later, as she found herself wandering along the streets of a city in Qinghai, she encountered a young fox boy with two different coloured eyes — one gold and one jade. Upon questioning the boy about his residence and parentage, she decided that it was time to pay a visit to an old friend.
