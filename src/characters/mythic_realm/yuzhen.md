<!-- section: introduction -->

A Qilin who served as [Jianqing's](/oc-gallery/characters/jianqing) lieutenant when he was still a general. Upon Jianqing’s ascendance to the imperial throne, Yuzhen was named the Grand Marshal for the role he played in overthrowing the previous tyrant emperor.<br><br>

While Qilins are typically gentle and pacifistic folk, Yuzhen is a cold and reserved individual, who sees himself as a soldier, first and foremost, a belief moulded by his cruel upbringing at the hands the previous dragon emperor. He is extremely stubborn and independent, but also fiercely loyal, particularly towards Jianqing.<br><br> 

At the same time, he is dutiful and disciplined, taking his responsibilities very seriously, even at the cost of his own well-being.
