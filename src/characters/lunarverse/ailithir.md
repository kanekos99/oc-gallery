<!-- section: introduction -->

Known as the Silver Sage of the Lunaris, Ailithir is a gentle and benevolent priest who serves as a vessel for his deity and a guiding light for his people. Patient and calm, he loves to offer help or advice, extending his compassion to everyone.<br><br>

When not found at the local temple, he lives together with his [boyfriend/ bodyguard](/oc-gallery/characters/caden/) and their adopted children in a small cabin by the sea.

<!-- section: story -->

<!-- sub-section: youth -->

Ailithir lived for the most of his youth within the Illuminated City, the capital of the Lunaris Halidom. By his birthright, he was chosen to become the next Silver Sage. As the first son of the prestigious and powerful Silver family, he led a happy and uneventful life, at least for the first ten years of his existence, studying and training under the former Silver Sage.<br><br>

However, when he was ten years old, his mother died giving birth to his youngest brother, Sakar. To make things worse, Sakar was born with pure black fur. His father became paranoid that Sakar's existence would be discovered, which would not only bring disgrace to their proud family name, but also put Sakar at risk of being murdered by superstitious Lunarians.<br><br>

He locked Sakar up in a small room within their mansion, charged Ailithir with the role of taking care of him, and made him promise to never let Sakar leave the house. Ailithir grew very close to Sakar, as he was the only person other than their father that Sakar had any interaction with.<br><br>

Ailithir often told Sakar stories of the outside world, which ignited a deep curiosity within the younger cat. When Sakar asked Ailithir to bring him out, Ailithir could not refuse. They did not wander far, and were soon found by their father. Their father, upon discovering that Ailithir had went against his wishes and put his brother's life at risk, flew into a rage and struck Ailithir. Ailithir, who previously had never been hit or punished, was deeply traumatized by this incident.<br><br>

Yet years later, Ailithir gave in to Sakar's demands to bring him outside once more since their father had left on a long trip and he was assured that no one would find out. However, in a moment of distraction, Ailithir was separated from Sakar. He searched desparately for Sakar, but could not find any trace of his brother. Eventually, he had no choice but to give up. He returned to the mansion, but told no one of what had happened, for he was certain that his father would kill him if he found out the truth. When Sakar's disappearance was discovered, Ailithir (though wrecked with guilt) lied and claimed that Sakar must have snuck out on his own. (Ailithir was 20 and Sakar was 10 then.)<br><br>

After Sakar's disappearance, his father grew even more despondent, sometimes neglecting his sons altogether. At other times, he was excessively strict and ill-tempered, such that even the smallest step out of line would incur his wrath.

<!-- sub-section: main story -->

Ailithir started spending more time away from home and in the company of his mentor, the former Silver Sage, who was a close family friend as well. Rumours of unrest within the neighbouring Rhyll Dominion began to spread, which culminated in a visit from the leaders of the Rhyll. The Rhyll asked for the Lunaris to spare them some land, for their territories were slowly being encroached on by a mysterious plague that left their earth barren and their water poisoned. However, the Lunaris declined their request, for their realm was already too small and would surely be overpopulated if they allowed the Rhyll to move in.<br><br>

Out of desperation, the Rhyll attacked the Lunaris realm, determined to seize the territory and save their people. As the Captain of the Lunaris Guard, Ailithir's father went away to join the war. However, the Lunaris forces were easily defeated by the far more combat-ready Rhyll. Eventually, the Rhyll army arrived at the Illuminated City, with Ailithir's father in tow as a prisoner. At the gates of the city, before an audience of horrified Lunaris (with Ailithir among them), they killed Ailithir's father and threatened the Lunaris to surrender, or they will do the same to the rest of them.<br><br>

The former Silver Sage ordered what was left of the Lunaris forces to defend the city, but it was a lost cause and the city was seized overnight. The Rhyll killed and imprisoned many members of the Lunaris noble families, but Ailithir was able to escape. Terrified, lost and completely helpless, Ailithir stumbled through the wilderness for weeks, trying to survive and evade capture.<br><br>

Eventually, Ailithir encountered a small group of deserters from the Rhyll Dominion, or rebels as they called themselves. They saved Ailithir from a Rhyll patrol and unaware of Ailithir's identity, they took him in, even though their leader, Caden, had his reservations. They traveled together for a few months, and Ailithir almost believed that he can start a new life with them, completely abandoning his devastated realm. However, mistakes were made and a majority of the group was captured by the Rhyll, leaving only Ailithir and Caden behind.<br><br>

Ailithir offered to help Caden to save his friends, and the two of them ventured to the nearest Rhyll outpost where they were sure the prisoners would be taken. Ailithir managed to help Caden's friends escape, but he gets captured himself. The Rhyll tortured him and demanded for him to tell them the location of a rumored secret Lunaris base, where a rebellion had begun. Ailithir, however, refused to tell them anything, so he was thrown back into a cell and left to await his execution.<br><br>

Caden arrived to save him and the two of them escape. Caden confronted Ailithir about his true identity, and Ailithir was forced to accept that he could not escape from his duties as the Silver Sage. He made up his mind to return and help the Lunaris drive out the Rhyll. Caden decided to come with him. Together, they headed to the secret Lunaris base, which was located on an offshore island, and indeed they found that what was left of the Lunaris forces had amassed there.<br><br>

Ailithir was reunited with some of his old friends who had escaped as well, but others were angry that Ailithir had abandoned them when their families were killed or captured. Ailithir enlisted Caden's experience in fighting the Rhyll and led several counterattacks against the Rhyll that were for the most part, victories, though they came at huge costs. Eventually, Ailithir learnt to harness Lunarian magical abilities for offensive attacks, which gave the Lunaris an edge over the Rhyll warriors. Soon, they won enough battles to carve their way back to the Illuminated City.<br><br>

Ailithir and Caden teamed up to defeat the Rhyll General leading the invasion, Ghost. However, with strong regeneration abilities, Ghost was incredibly difficult to kill, refusing to die even after they had beheaded him. Since their leader was not dead, the rest of the Rhyll troops refused to surrender. To make things worse, the Rhyll High General had arrived with reinforcements in his wake. Tired of fighting and causing more bloodshed, Ailithir negotiated a deal with the High General, offering part of the Lunaris's land up to the Rhyll, much to the outrage of some of his Lunarian allies.<br><br>

The High General refused and instead, asked for the Lunaris's ships, such that he might bring what's left of his people to seek out new lands where they can settle down. Ailithir acquiesced to his demands readily. With no cause to fight for anymore, Ghost finally put himself to rest, ending the war between the realms.<br><br>

While most of the Rhyll departed from the continent, a portion, consisting largely of the elderly and the sick, remained behind. Ailithir constructed a small secluded village for them on Lunarian territory and promised to try and find a cure for the plague.<br><br>

Caden decided to remain with Ailithir instead of leaving to travel on his own again, much to Ailithir's gratitude. They confessed their feelings for each other, but decided to keep their relationship a secret for fear of outcry from the Lunarian population.

<!-- sub-section: future -->

After the war, Ailithir placed all his efforts into helping to rebuild his realm. Eventually, the realm started to prosper once more, entering a new golden age.<br><br> 

Ailithir, finally able to return to his more passive role of being a priest, rebuilt his mansion into an orphanage for children orphaned during the war, and adopted a young orphaned girl by the name of Hilda.
