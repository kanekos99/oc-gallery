<!-- section: introduction -->

A former warrior of the Rhyll Dominion, he grew disillusioned with a life of battle and left to seek a better future elsewhere. However, by deserting his realm, he is branded a traitor and hunted down by his own kin.<br><br>

Caden helped [Ailithir](/oc-gallery/characters/ailithir) save the Lunaris realm by driving out the invading Rhyll forces. Ironically, he was then made the Captain of the Lunaris Guard, but he eventually resigned and became a baker.<br><br>

He has no magical powers whatsoever unlike his Lunaris allies, but he is very skilled in melee combat.
