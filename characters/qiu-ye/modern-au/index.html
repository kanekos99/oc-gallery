It was common knowledge among the employees of Qinghai Technologies that Qiu Ye was a terrible secretary who was really good at his job.

Terrible, being that he was basically never at his desk and could, instead, frequently be found either chatting up the baristas at the cafe downstairs, or taking a nap on the most comfortable sofa in his boss's office. He would also clock off at 5pm sharp every day, claiming that he needed to pick his young son up from school.

Still, he got all his work done, and no matter how last minute it was, he never missed a single deadline. So everyone admitted, begrudgingly, that he was, at his very least, competent, and at his very best, nothing short of a miracle worker. After all, he was the only one who could convince the boss to approve the most ludicrous proposals, and subsequently quell his temper if they turn out exactly as poorly as expected.

That wasn't to say he never got into trouble, but in a situation where any other employee would have been dismissed instantly, he would receive nothing more than a slap on the wrist. And thus, the rumor mill churned, eventually drawing to the scandalous conclusion that Qiu Ye was most probably sleeping together with the boss.

When the fox spirit caught wind of that rumor, he just laughed and said, "Are you serious? You took that long to figure it out? I don't even think I tried to be subtle about it."
