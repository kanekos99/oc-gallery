Warning: Mentions of dark topics like murder, abuse, captivity and mild dub-con

An AU where everything is dark and fucked up so you probably get the idea.

Like in the original, Caden is war veteran. But he turned to alcoholism to cope with the lingering trauma of the war and the grief of losing many of his closest friends.

Unlike the original Caden who is just slightly grumpy, this guy has some serious anger issues and violent impulses.

He started a co-dependent relationship with Ailithir, since they were all each other had left. However, Ailithir began to display increasingly erratic and self-destructive behaviour, which frustrated and worried him greatly. Until one day Caden was no longer able to tolerate it and struck Ailithir out of anger.

At first he was horrified by what he had done, but when Ailithir only laughed and asked him to hit him again, Caden lost his temper.

From that point onwards, he became physically and verbally abusive towards Ailithir, partially out of annoyance towards Ailithir's behavior, yet also partially because he felt like he did not deserve Ailithir's love anymore and wanted to drive him away. However, Ailithir forgave him each time and never left him.

One day, Caden was approached by group who claimed to be dissidents of the Silver Sage. They exposed all of Ailithir's crimes to Caden - all of the dark rituals, killings, and child sacrifices he had been conducting in secret. They then asked for his help to put a stop to Ailithir's madness. As Caden was the only person Ailithir trusted, he was the only one who could get close enough to him and kill him.

Caden, who felt pure horror and disgust upon learning the truth, agreed to ally himself with their cause. He immediately confronted Ailithir and tried to kill him, but was ultimately unable to bring himself to do the deed.

As Caden attempted to flee after his failed assassination attempt, Ailithir overpowered him and knocked him out.

When he woke up again, Caden found himself in a dim cellar, chained next to an altar. He could only watch helplessly as guards dragged in the members of that group of dissidents who had sought out his help. One by one, upon that blood-stained altar, Ailithir slit their throats.

Caden expected that he would soon share their fate, yet Ailithir only ordered the guards to take him back to a cell. Afterwards, Ailithir would visit him every day and act all caring and affectionate towards him, much to Caden's increasing discomfort.

  • He lost his eye during the war and covers it up with an eyepatch.
