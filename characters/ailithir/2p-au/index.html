Warning: Mentions of dark topics like murder, abuse and captivity

An AU where everything is dark and fucked up so you probably get the idea. Ailithir is pretty much a villain in this AU.

In this AU, the storyline follows the same as the original except Ailithir ends up becoming increasingly unstable and despondent after losing almost all of his family and friends during the war. He turns to dark magic and ends up becoming a cult leader, sacrificing children to a dark god who promised to bring his loved ones back to life.

However, weighed down by the immense guilt of his actions and his past failures, he also begins engage in self-destructive behaviour as a way to punish himself, displaying extremely masochistic tendencies.

At the same time, he becomes completely obsessive over the one person he still cares about, Caden, and will do whatever it takes to keep Caden by his side forever.

Caden

 Captive/ Lover(?)

While they started out happy and loving in the beginning, their relationship has devolved into a toxic mess. Caden is physically and verbally abusive to Ailithir. Although Ailithir is deeply hurt by Caden's cruelty, he suffers through it all because he believes that he deserves it. However, after Caden turns on him and attempts (but fails) to kill him, Ailithir ends up locking him in the cellar of the temple and keeping him as a captive. Not as retaliation for the assassination attempt, but because he feared Caden was going to leave him for good.
