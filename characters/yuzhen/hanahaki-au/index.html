Yuzhen is a bodyguard, who is in love with his boss, Jianqing. Unfortunately, he is unable to confess his feelings to Jianqing, who is already dating and engaged to someone else (his secretary and Yuzhen's friend, Qiu Ye) and Yuzhen gets the Hanahaki Disease because of it.

(This is a dumb AU that I made for a zine-making workshop. The setting here is basically the same as the Modern AU except for the fact that the Hanahaki Disease exists. I made a separate page for this AU because I did not want to introduce the existence of the Hanahaki Disease into the regular Modern AU.)
