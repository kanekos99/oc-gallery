Yuzhen

  • Lin Yuzhen (林玉珍)
  • Male
  • Qilin
  • 19+
  • Qinghai
A Dance of Autumn Leaves
profile

A Qilin who served as Jianqing's lieutenant when he was still a general. Upon Jianqing’s ascendance to the imperial throne, Yuzhen was named the Grand Marshal for the role he played in overthrowing the previous tyrant emperor.

While Qilins are typically gentle and pacifistic folk, Yuzhen is a cold and reserved individual, who sees himself as a soldier, first and foremost, a belief moulded by his cruel upbringing at the hands the previous dragon emperor. He is extremely stubborn and independent, but also fiercely loyal, particularly towards Jianqing.

At the same time, he is dutiful and disciplined, taking his responsibilities very seriously, even at the cost of his own well-being.

  • Like most other Qilin, he is vegetarian. Ingesting meat would make him violently ill. He does not like to eat anything with excessive oil or spices either.
  • His favourite food is camellia flowers, especially ones that are freshly plucked from the stem.
  • He has two swords - a standard issue steel sword for fighting, and a decorative sword made of pure jade, which was a gift from Jianqing.
  • He can disguise himself in shadows and travel through them quickly, which proves to be useful for espionage missions.
  • He doesn’t particularly like physical touch as well

Jianqing

Boss/ Friend

As Jianqing's loyal bodyguard and lieutenant, Yuzhen sees him as the epitome of a fair and just ruler, a kind-hearted and noble individual whom he has sworn to serve dutifully for the rest of his life. It is for that reason that he has vowed to take the secret feelings he harbors for Jianqing to his grave.

Qiu Ye

Close Friend

“I would rather eat a whole cow than become friends with the likes of you.” Despite that platitude Yuzhen once uttered, they ended up becoming friends. After all, he promised Jianqing that he would protect Qiu Ye.
