profile

Jianqing is the rich and handsome CEO of a tech company called Qinghai Technologies. They um... *spins wheel* ...make water purifiers. He inherited the company at the tender age of 27 after his father's untimely demise.

When he was 18 years old, he dropped out of college and ran away from home to be a bum because he was too stressed out by his family's expectations blah blah blah. He ended up homeless and and alone on the streets. He was later picked up by a male host by the name of Qiu Ye, who offered him a place to stay.

He lived together with Qiu Ye for three years and fell in love with him. The two of them became involved in a casual relationship. However, shortly after that, Qiu Ye suddenly disappeared without any warning.

Heartbroken, Jianqing returned home with his tail between his legs and vowed to become a rich and successful billionaire so that he can track down his missing boyfriend.

Right after he was officially enstated as the CEO of Qinghai Technologies, Jianqing suddenly received an unexpected application for the position of his personal secretary. The applicant turned out to be none other than Qiu Ye. Jianqing immediately agreed to hire him.

Qiu Ye was missing for six years, but he refused to tell Jianqing what happened to him, feigning ignorance and claiming that he had to deal with some vague family matters. Later, Jianqing also found out that Qiu Ye now had a six year old son by the name of Qiu Zhi, and Qiu Ye claimed he did not know who the other parent was.

Still, being the simp that he was, Jianqing decided not to pursue the matter and resumed his relationship with Qiu Ye, even offering to be his son's stepfather. For some time, he lived blissfully unaware of the truth.

Qiu Ye was actually a spy sent by a rival company to steal corporate secrets from him.

(But he also did genuinely have feelings for Jianqing and he only agreed to be a spy because the CEO of the rival company, who happened to be Jianqing's older half-brother, was holding his son hostage. Or something along those lines.)

Qiu Ye

 Secretary/ Lover

Jianqing adores him and is so infatuated with him that he lets him get away with almost anything. Even when Jianqing appears to be mad, he's never actually angry at Qiu Ye. Being the domineering boss is just a fun roleplay for him and he's sure Qiu Ye likes it as well. But deep down, he is very scared of losing Qiu Ye again and is willing to do anything to keep Qiu Ye by his side.
