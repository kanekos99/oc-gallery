profile

A mysterious white dragon who serves as a personal attendant to one of the four Divine Lords, the White Tiger King of Baiyan. Although he is not mute, he rarely speaks to anyone. Little is known about his past, not even his true name, as he is merely addressed affectionately as "Xiao Yu" (or little jade) by the king.

  • He is blind in his right eye and his right horn is broken.
