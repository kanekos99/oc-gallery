profile

The former Dragon Emperor and Jianqing’s older half-brother. A tyrant who wrested power unjustly, only to later become disillusioned with ruling after his actions drove away everyone he cared about. To remedy his isolation and loneliness, he takes pleasure in tormenting those he considers to be beneath him.

His subjects believe him to be cruel and uncaring, but it is the occasional shred of compassion he demonstrates that instils more fear. What sort of man would adopt an orphaned young Qilin, only to abuse him and force him into a life of violence and bloodshed? How could one avoid the wrath of an emperor who seems to act purely according his whims?

Only Jianqing knows of the gentle older brother who once held his hand and patiently taught him how to read and write, even when all of the imperial tutors had given up on the unruly second prince. The same brother who always shouldered the blame for him whenever he got into trouble, who would be unfairly beaten for infractions he did not commit. The same brother whose heart he pierced with his sword, putting an end to his reign of tyranny.

  • Ice magic: Lianyu inherited the ability to wield ice magic from his ice dragon heritage. His ice magic is extremely powerful, and as such he relies on it as his main form of offence. He can summon projectiles, blades and spears of ice. He can also form weapons out of ice, such as shields or an ice sword for him to fly on. His strongest ability is to summon giant curved blades of ice that resemble a lotus flower, piercing his opponent and entombing them in a frozen cage.
  • Minor water magic: Lianyu can also manipulate water to a certain extent, but not particularly well. He always ends up unintentionally freezing it into a solid state.
  • Martial arts: Lianyu is trained in martial arts and can wield a sword or a spear competently. However, he rarely carries an actual weapon and prefers to form them from ice. He also utilises an ice whip sometimes.

  • He likes peach tea and lotus flower pastries.
  • He doesn't like alcohol and is a lightweight, but he still forces himself to drink for formal occasions.
  • He spends at least an hour every morning polishing the scales on his tail.
  • Whenever he gets angry or distressed, his surroundings will drop in temperature.

Jianqing

 Brother/ Enemy

Lianyu shares a complicated relationship with his younger brother, Jianqing. While they were close in their youth, Lianyu eventually came to resent Jianqing, for he would be the one punished for Jianqing's mistakes. He also became intensely jealous of the favour Jianqing received just because he was the son of the Empress, rather than a lowly concubine. Yet deep down, he still cares about Jianqing and is unable to bring himself to get rid of his brother once and for all, as Jianqing is the only family he has left.

Qiu Ye

 Former captive/ Enemy

Lianyu once coveted the love and devotion Qiu Ye displayed towards his brother, Jianqing. He hence brainwashed Qiu Ye into thinking they were lovers. However, he has no real love for Qiu Ye and only wanted to possess him because he was precious to Jianqing. He considers Qiu Ye to be a lowly fox spirit who is beneath him. When Qiu Ye repeatedly defies him and rejects his favour, Lianyu doesn’t hesitate to make his life miserable.
